1. Who we are

Docsmith is a report designer for Oracle APEX, developed and operated by Gowryshangar Balasubramaniyam in Sri Lanka ("Docsmith", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for anything about privacy: bgowryshangar@gmail.com.

We follow Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025), whose rules on processing personal data apply from 1 January 2027, and, for visitors and customers in the European Union or United Kingdom, the GDPR / UK GDPR principles where they apply.

3. Data inside the Docsmith software

Docsmith is installed in your Oracle database and runs on your servers. The reports, SQL queries, report data, user names and logs it stores live in your database, under your control. We cannot access them, and the software does not send usage data or "call home".

  • Licence checks happen inside the render server, offline. With your own render server, your licence key and Instance ID are never sent to us.
  • Own render server: report data travels only between your database and your render server.
  • Hosted render server (optional service): your database sends the data of one document to our server, the server creates the PDF, Word or Excel file, returns it and keeps nothing. Report data is processed in memory only and is not written to disk; the server log records only the report code, number of pages and time taken. For this service you are the controller and we act as your processor; a data processing agreement is available on request.

For the people whose data appears in your reports (for example your employees or customers), your organisation remains responsible under data protection law.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use a small number of service providers who process data for us under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive): e-mail, the list of trial requests and the download files.
  • n8n, running on our own virtual server hosted by Hostinger: receives the trial form and sends the e-mails.
  • Railway: hosts this website and, if you use it, the hosted render server.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the fonts used on this website (your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address).
  • Payment providers and our accountant, when you buy a licence.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, or to protect our rights in a dispute.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Sri Lanka, for example in the United States or the European Union. We only use established providers that protect data with security measures and contractual safeguards, and we limit what we send them to what each service needs.

7. How long we keep it

  • Trial requests: 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer or ask us to delete them earlier.
  • Customer and licence records: while you are a customer, and then for up to 6 years for accounting, tax and licence-verification purposes.
  • Support e-mails: up to 3 years after the issue is closed.
  • Hosted render server: report data is not retained after the document is returned.
  • Website technical logs: kept by the hosting provider for a short period (usually up to 30 days).

8. Security

We protect personal data with access control and two-step sign-in on the accounts that hold it, encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the website and the hosted render server, and by keeping as little data as possible. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold data about you and give you a copy;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it, or stop or limit how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent, for example to follow-up e-mails, at any time (this does not affect what we did before);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests.

Write to bgowryshangar@gmail.com. We will reply within 21 working days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. Every follow-up e-mail also lets you simply reply "stop".

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka, or to the data protection authority where you live.

10. Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools. Your browser only loads the page itself, its images and the Google Fonts files.

If we add analytics or other cookies in future, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

11. Children

Docsmith is a business product. Our website and services are not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
